# Night Shift explainer media

60 fps, 1920×1080 H.264 clips and PNG stills that explain how the game is built. They are
shown on [how-its-made.html](../how-its-made.html) (served from GitHub Pages) and regenerated
with the scripts in `scripts/capture/` (see `scripts/capture/README.md`).

| Clip | Length | Size | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [01-flight-detection.mp4](01-flight-detection.mp4) | 24.8 s | 4.2 MB | Inertial steering toward your finger, braking into a hover, then the L3 police cruiser's headlights find us: the fuse fills in exactly 60 frames, Exhibit A develops, and we respawn at the checkpoint. |
| [02-flight-hazards.mp4](02-flight-hazards.mp4) | 41.1 s | 11.2 MB | The cruiser, a porch light arming, clicking on and catching us (Exhibit A: doorbell camera), Mrs. Pruitt's ghost cone and snap, the helicopter spotlight, a rooftop camera beside a flickering office window, the crane boom, pigeons and the Afterburner dash, each outlined with its real hit shape from the hazard code. |
| [03-deep-shift.mp4](03-deep-shift.mp4) | 43.0 s | 11.5 MB | A whole L3 shift played by the autopilots through the real mouse: three specimens in any order, off-screen arrows and the minimap, one sunrise clock across flight and abduction, checkpoints that move with each delivery, and the Specimen Report. |
| [04-interiors.mp4](04-interiors.mp4) | 36.7 s | 9.8 MB | The verlet ragdoll and its 21 contact circles, the beam moved by relative drag, walls built round each room with openings cut in, the camera following the specimen through Coach Barb's house, and the tiled backdrops. |

| [06-sleepers-noise.mp4](06-sleepers-noise.mp4) | 35.3 s | 9.2 MB | Brenda, Tiny and Biscuit each hear within a radius, and noise falls off with distance. Their wake meters appear as live readouts until we wake Biscuit on purpose and get the failure card. |
| [07-beatable.mp4](07-beatable.mp4) | 34.7 s | 7.5 MB | The flight autopilot's exposure map and plan against a beeline that gets spotted, then the abduction pilot with its finger landing 3 frames late against a straight drag at the exit that bonks Gary awake in under 3 s, and the numbers from the headless vitest proofs. |
