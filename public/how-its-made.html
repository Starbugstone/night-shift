Night Shift: how it's made

Eight short films about how the game works, captured from the real game at 60 fps. Each frame is exactly 1/60 s of game time: the capture replaces the browser's clock, so a slow machine can't drop or stretch frames.

The overlays are drawn from the live simulation (hit shapes, colliders, camera cones, wake meters), so what you see outlined is what the code tests. Saucer and specimen are driven with real mouse input, by hand or by the game's own autopilots.
