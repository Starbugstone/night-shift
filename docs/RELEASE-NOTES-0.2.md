# Night Shift v0.2 · "Temporal Dispatch"

*Draft, 2026-11-27. Built from `git log origin/main..preprod` (69 commits since v0.1) and GDD-11 to GDD-14.
The release coordinator updates it before the merge to main.*

The Bureau has a time machine now, and a lot more paperwork. v0.2 adds five shifts, which makes
fourteen. It ends the season in 2199 and gives every town a new way in.

## New shifts

- **Birch Lane, two short calls before Maple Court.** *Baker Mabel's First Batch* has one baker, one
  motion light and a cat that is asleep on the job. *While the Hendersons Are Away* brings two sleepers,
  a shared sunrise clock and Mrs. Pruitt's binoculars. Wait for her to look away.
- **Ni! (Grimsby Keep, 1122).** Krix wants the Holy Grail so OBELISK™ can sell eternal life by the
  month. Collect three sleeping witnesses past taunting battlements, hostile cows and a Black Knight who
  is fine, really. The knights by the west hedge want a shrubbery.
- **The War of the Colds (Route 9 Drive-In, 1953).** A tripod with a cold staggers across the screen
  and sneezes a heat ray, and the front rows applaud. Bring in three witnesses before the credits.
  Somebody needs that missing tripod leg back.
- **Your Town Is in the Way (Grimsby, 2199).** This is the season finale. Grimsby is in the way of a
  hyperspace bypass. Get three residents onto the census before the demolition fleet arrives. Slip past
  the poetry guards, don't panic, and bring a towel.
- **Temporal Dispatch.** Clearing 1955 opens 1122 and 1953, in either order. 2199 opens once both are
  delivered. After the finale, every delivered date stays replayable, and so does the finale scene.

## Trophy room

- Every new souvenir, town pickup and specimen has a shelf, with English and French names and
  descriptions.
- Each specimen in the Specimen Archive has its own probe notes.
- Missing exhibits now offer **Go get it**, which takes you to the shift that has them.

## Every town, reworked

- New openings: you now arrive somewhere different in each town, and the trouble is on the way rather
  than parked on the launch pad. Main Street, the City Centre, St. Mildred's, the SS Buffet Royale,
  Patent Valley and 1955 all changed.
- The detection fuse now gets steadily shorter through the season, and Maple Court is a little gentler.
- Nine abduction rooms have longer routes, with new turns and floors. Two cruise ship routes are longer too.
- Solid furniture has an outline, so you can tell what you will bump into from what is only decoration.

## Sound

- Gameplay cues sound more like a cartoon. The ambient loops are gone.
- The tractor beam is softer, and the snoring is quieter and rarer.

## Fixes and polish

- The French text has had a full pass and reads as French, not as translated paperwork. The captain's log
  stays « log du capitaine », with a stardate.
- Mabel's dough bowl no longer looks like the cherry pie.
- The title screen shows the version again: **v0.2**.
