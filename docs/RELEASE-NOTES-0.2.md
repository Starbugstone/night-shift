# Night Shift v0.2 · "Temporal Dispatch"

The Bureau's time machine is busy, and the paperwork has multiplied. Version 0.2 adds five shifts —
fourteen in all — taking Agent Zib from a sleepy bakery to the end of the world (Grimsby's, anyway).

## Five new shifts

Two short live calls on Birch Lane now bridge Spin City and Maple Court:

- **Baker Mabel's First Batch (Birch Lane Bakery).** Sneak past one motion light and a cat who
  sleeps on the keys.
- **While the Hendersons Are Away (Birch Lane).** Two sleepers share one sunrise, and Mrs. Pruitt
  is watching through her binoculars. Wait for her to look away.

Clear **Clocking Out at 10:04 (1955)** to open the new Temporal Dispatch dates — 1122 and 1953 in
either order, then the finale:

- **Ni! (Grimsby Keep, 1122).** Krix wants the Holy Grail so OBELISK™ can sell eternal life by the
  month. Bring in three sleeping witnesses past taunting battlements, flying cows, a killer rabbit
  and a Black Knight who is fine, really.
- **The War of the Colds (Route 9 Drive-In, 1953).** A tripod with a cold staggers across the
  screen and sneezes a heat ray. The front rows applaud. Cross after the sneeze.
- **Your Town Is in the Way (Grimsby, 2199).** The season finale. Grimsby is in the way of a
  hyperspace bypass, and three residents need to be on the census before the demolition fleet
  arrives. Slip past the poetry guards, don't panic, and bring a towel.

Once the finale is delivered, every date stays open to replay — and so does the Bureau epilogue.

## Town pickups

Every new shift hides one: a cherry pie, a garden gnome, a shrubbery, a tripod leg and a towel. The
shift memo always hints where to look. If you filed a pickup on a shift you didn't finish, the
trophy room offers **Bring it home for ★** to take you back for its star.

## Trophy room

- Every new souvenir, town pickup and specimen has its own place on the shelves, with a name and a
  description.
- Every specimen in the Specimen Archive now has their own probe note.
- An empty shelf offers **Go get it**, taking you straight to the shift that hides it.

## Ranks

The career ladder now spans every star in the game, so a clean run earns a promotion every order or
two. Three new top ranks wait at the summit: *Fleet Admiral of Naps*, *Chief Paradox Officer* and
*Employee of the Millennium*. Finish the finale to find out whether you finally get a parking ring.

## Every town, reworked

- You now arrive somewhere different in each town, and the trouble waits on the way rather than on
  the launch pad.
- The spotting fuse tightens steadily through the season, and Maple Court is a little gentler.
- Eleven abduction routes are longer, with new turns and floors, and souvenirs are easier to snag on
  the way out.
- Solid furniture now wears a cream outline, so you can tell what you'll bonk into from pure decor.

## Learning as you go

Each new hazard is introduced the first time you meet it, in the shift memo or with an on-screen
tip: the sneezing tripod, the killer rabbit, the crane boom, the flickering office windows, the
rooftop cameras and more. Tips stay up long enough to read, and room tips appear along the route you
are actually taking.

## Sound and art

- Gameplay sounds are softer and more cartoony, and the background loops are gone.
- The tractor beam whooshes once instead of constantly, and snoring is quieter and rarer.
- Ticking clocks, bell clappers and cows only make noise when it actually concerns you.
- Stretched props were redrawn at their real shapes.

## Saves

Your v0.1 progress carries over. A damaged or empty save no longer looks like a fresh start: the
game keeps whatever progress it can still read, from an older save if it has to. When a file came
back damaged, the title screen says so ("Your file got chewed. We kept what we could.", or "File
chewed to bits. Here’s a fresh one." when nothing could be read), and **Reset progress** stays
within reach, behind its confirmation card.

## Fixed

- Shifts that don't end at sunrise (1955 and the 2199 demolition) no longer say "before dawn", and
  their clock displays properly everywhere.
- Route labels no longer cover a cable or a detector edge.
- Exit signs stay on screen in French too, and shift memos never run into the performance goals.
- Vrell's close-call remarks now match the seconds shown on the report.
- The title screen now follows the release version automatically (**v0.2**).
