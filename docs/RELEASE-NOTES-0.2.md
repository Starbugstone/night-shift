# Night Shift v0.2 · "Temporal Dispatch"

The Bureau has a time machine now, and a lot more paperwork. Version 0.2 adds five shifts, for fourteen
in all, and takes Agent Zib from a sleepy bakery to the end of the world (Grimsby's end, anyway).

## Five new shifts

- **Birch Lane, two small jobs before Maple Court.** In *Baker Mabel's First Batch*, sneak past one
  motion light and a cat that is asleep on the job. In *While the Hendersons Are Away*, two sleepers
  share one sunrise and Mrs. Pruitt is watching through her binoculars. Wait for her to look away.
- **1955 opens the Temporal Dispatch.** Clear it, and 1122 and 1953 open, in either order.
- **Ni! (Grimsby Keep, 1122).** Krix wants the Holy Grail so OBELISK™ can sell eternal life by the
  month. Bring in three sleeping witnesses past taunting battlements, flying cows, a killer rabbit and a
  Black Knight who is fine, really.
- **The War of the Colds (Route 9 Drive-In, 1953).** A tripod with a cold staggers across the screen
  and sneezes a heat ray. The front rows applaud. Cross after the sneeze.
- **Your Town Is in the Way (Grimsby, 2199).** The season finale. Grimsby is in the way of a hyperspace
  bypass, and three residents need to be on the census before the demolition fleet arrives. Slip past
  the poetry guards, don't panic, and bring a towel.

Once the finale is delivered, every date stays open to replay, and so does the ending.

## Town pickups

Every new shift hides one: a cherry pie, a garden gnome, a shrubbery, a missing tripod leg and a towel.
The shift memo always hints where to look. If you filed a pickup on a shift you didn't finish, the
trophy room keeps a button to go back for its star.

## Trophy room

- Every new souvenir, town pickup and specimen has its own place on the shelves, with a name and a
  description.
- Each specimen in the Specimen Archive has their own probe notes.
- An empty shelf offers **Go get it**, which takes you straight to the shift that has it.

## Ranks

The career ladder now runs across every star in the game, so a perfect player earns a promotion every
order or two. Three new top ranks wait at the end: Fleet Admiral of Naps, Chief Paradox Officer and Employee
of the Millennium. Finish the finale to find out whether you finally get a parking ring.

## Every town, reworked

- You now arrive somewhere different in each town, and the trouble sits on the way rather than on the
  launch pad.
- The detection fuse gets steadily shorter through the season, and Maple Court is a little gentler.
- Nine abduction rooms and two cruise ship routes are longer, with new turns and floors.
- Solid furniture has a cream outline, so you can tell what you'll bump into from what is only
  decoration.

## Learning as you go

Each new danger is explained the first time you meet it, in the shift memo or with a tip on screen:
the sneezing tripod, the killer rabbit, the crane boom, the flickering office windows, the rooftop
cameras and more. Tips stay up long enough to read, and a room's tip shows on the
way you are actually going.

## Sound and art

- Gameplay sounds are softer and more cartoony. The background loops are gone.
- The tractor beam whooshes once instead of all the time, and snoring is quieter and rarer.
- Ticking clocks, bell clappers and cows only make noise when it matters to you.
- Artie, Penny and Moe have their own arms and legs, stretched props were redrawn, and Mabel's dough bowl
  no longer looks like the cherry pie.

## Saves

Your v0.1 progress carries over. A damaged or empty save no longer hides your older progress: the game
finds the last good one.

## Fixed

- Shifts that end at another deadline than sunrise (1955 and the 2199 demolition) no longer say
  "before dawn", and their clock looks right everywhere.
- The 2199 poetry cards stay up long enough to read.
- Sounds that repeated over and over now play only when something happens.
- A broken save no longer looks like a fresh start.
- Route labels no longer hide a cable or the edge of a detector's beam.
- Exit signs stay on screen in French too, and memos never run into the goals.
- The report's seconds match the time row.
- Slower hands get more time on the 1122 and 2199 clocks.
- The title screen shows the version again: **v0.2**.
