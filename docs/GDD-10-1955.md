## The idea (the user's)

Lightning hits the courthouse clock at 10:04 pm. The optional time car speeds east along
Courthouse Row, past the face of the tower. Its rear hook meets the cable stretched across the
road as lightning strikes. The car vanishes in a white bloom and two lines of fire carry on
down the street. Intercept the car from the west turnout to earn a fourth star. Abduct the
inventor at the clock tower, the bully at the coffee shop and the bandleader at the school dance.
Every souvenir is a nod to the film.

## The time car (town pickup, optional 4th star)

| Time | Clock | Event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 s | 10:03:40 | Car waits facing east at (170, 755), headlights on and countdown visible when in view. Zib points to the west turnout at (320, 850). |
| 13 s | 10:03:53 | Launch east at 70 px/s. The chip switches to the strike deadline, and the first arcs appear. |
| 15 s | 10:03:55 | The mint lock opens at (310, 755). Merge from the turnout, then match the car's eastbound speed. A cubic speed ramp brings bigger and brighter arcs as the car approaches the courthouse. |
| 20 s | 10:04:00 | Lightning hits the tower and stops its hands. The car's centre reaches (985, 755) at 330 px/s, with its rear hook at the cross-street cable (903, 755). A missed car vanishes in a white bloom. Two tyre trails race east ahead of it over 0.4 s and stay on the road. |

- Caught: the car is beamed up, the lightning still strikes the empty tower, no fire trails.
- Missed: no retry in this shift. It loops to L2's scorched time car behind Spin City.
- Over 20 car-first attempts, casual catches 60–85% and skilled catches at least 90%. Naive
  controllers miss at every tested delay. The lock takes 0.75 s within 40 px/s of the car's velocity.
- The lightning flash is spectacle: it doesn't raise suspicion or wake anyone (a global detector would
  make the safe route a lottery).
- Look: an unmistakable likeness of the first film's time machine (stainless wedge, gull-wing doors,
  black rear louvres, rear-deck machinery, side coils that glow when charging), with no badges, brand
  lettering or plate text ([reference sheet](l9/concepts/time-car.svg)). The user asked for it to look
  exactly like the car.
- The memo hints at it (hard rule): "A gull-wing time car is due at the clock tower at 10:04 sharp…"

## Story and humour (Neon)

Vrell slips Zib through the mothballed Temporal Dispatch (Basement B-4) because an unlicensed
1.21-gigawatt spike shows a human time machine with no Form 88-MPH, and Krix's lawyers want to patent
time travel the moment lightning hits the clock. Bureau paperwork against time travel; the film's lines
bent, not quoted ("Where we're napping, no pillows…"); the French copy uses the French dub's jokes
(2,21 gigowatts, 88 miles à l'heure, Nom de Zeus, convecteur). Replace Zib's "Don't step on a
butterfly" opener (another story's time-travel joke) with a nod to this film.
