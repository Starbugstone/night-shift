## The idea (the user's)

A 1955 town minutes before lightning hits the courthouse clock at 10:04 pm. The town pickup is the time
car: it speeds down Main Street to hook the clock-tower cable as the lightning strikes and vanishes,
leaving two fire trails on the road. Beam it up first: reachable, not easy. Abduct the inventor at the
clock tower, the bully at the coffee shop and the bandleader at the school dance. Every souvenir is a
nod to the film.

## The shift

- Unlocks after L8 (Temporal Dispatch). Work Order #0009.
- The shift clock is the town clock: **10:03:40 pm to 10:10:00 pm**, 380 s of play, one real second per
  clock second (the user chose a longer shift than Sol's draft, which ended at 10:08). The lightning
  strikes at **10:04:00** (20 s in) whatever the player does.
- Stars: Delivered all 3 by 10:10 · Ghost · Night owl (120 s to spare, by 10:08:00; tune to the band) ·
  **Time car** (4th).
- Story copy says "before the storm passes / by 10:10", never "before dawn" (Neon's draft used dawn;
  replace those lines, including the sunrise fail memo, with a 10:10 "storm's over, pass expired" line).

## The time car (town pickup, optional 4th star)

| Time | Clock | Event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 s | 10:03:40 | Car waits in its bay at (820, 1740), headlights on, countdown chip; Zib points at the west turnout. |
| 13 s | 10:03:53 | Launch north at 70 px/s; the chip switches to the strike deadline. |
| 15 s | 10:03:55 | The moving mint lock ring opens; the car accelerates (52 px/s²). Intercept from the west between y1560 and y1370; match its pace to fill the lock. |
| 20 s | 10:04:00 | Lightning hits the tower (clock hands stop). An uncollected car reaches the cable at 330 px/s and vanishes; two fire trails stay on the road (decor only, never a detector). |

- Caught: the car is beamed up, the lightning still strikes the empty tower, no fire trails.
- Missed: no retry in this shift. It loops to L2's scorched time car behind Spin City.
- Fair but not easy: over 20 car-first attempts, casual ≥ 60 % and skilled ≥ 90 % catches; the naive
  controllers catch it at no tested delay. New difficulty checks (Sol's plan, section 6).
- The lightning flash is spectacle: it doesn't raise suspicion or wake anyone (a global detector would
  make the safe route a lottery).
- Look: an unmistakable likeness of the first film's time machine (stainless wedge, gull-wing doors,
  black rear louvres, rear-deck machinery, side coils that glow when charging), with no badges, brand
  lettering or plate text ([reference sheet](l9/concepts/time-car.svg)). The user asked for it to look
  exactly like the car.
- The memo hints at it (hard rule): "A gull-wing time car is due at the clock tower at 10:04 sharp…"

## Story and humour (Neon)

Vrell slips Zib through the mothballed Temporal Dispatch (Basement B-4) because an unlicensed
1.21-gigawatt spike shows a human time machine with no Form 88-MPH, and Krix's lawyers want to patent
time travel the moment lightning hits the clock. Bureau paperwork against time travel; the film's lines
bent, not quoted ("Where we're napping, no pillows…"); the French copy uses the French dub's jokes
(2,21 gigowatts, 88 miles à l'heure, Nom de Zeus, convecteur). Replace Zib's "Don't step on a
butterfly" opener (another story's time-travel joke) with a nod to this film.
