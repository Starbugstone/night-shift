## First pass · Souvenir reliability and cruise ship routes

The flex capacitor went from **0/20 to 20/20** casual souvenir deliveries. The ordinary route still passed it by on all 20 seeds. Seeking it added a median **1.7 seconds** to that same player's successful exit.

The old pickup sat at x=460 on the left of the clock riser, 141 logical pixels from the planned climb at x=601. The chest had to come within 57 pixels horizontally. The lightning cable swung between Doc and the shelf. On the quiet-rules proof, retrieving the capacitor took 15.1 extra seconds, 30 bonks and 100 peak wake. With wake failures enabled, 16 casual attempts woke Doc and the remaining four missed the item.

The new shelf sits on the right. Its item centre is x=687 and its base is y=−377, above the cable. A player can lean right after the shutter and rejoin the climb. The quiet-rules proof now takes 6.7 extra seconds with two bonks and nine peak wake.

A second cause affected multiple levels. A boot or hand could visibly touch a souvenir outside the chest's pickup window. A souvenir could also trigger the pickup sound and still snag behind a door or trail outside the delivery radius. Moving the beam now collects an item touched by a ragdoll joint as well as one in its existing collection area. A captured souvenir floats along and gets credited on successful delivery. Aborts and failures earn nothing.

### How to read the survey

The same 20 deterministic seeds ran on the baseline (`800c04c`) and on the change. There are 65 registered room routes plus Field Check. Each seed ran the casual model twice with the room's real rules and no upgrades. On the ordinary run the hand followed the exit route. On the souvenir run it took one side trip from the closest approach to the item and returned to the route. Both runs used the standard reaction delay, hand wobble, hazard phases and snag recovery.

**Grabbed** is the share that registered a pickup. **Home** is the share that delivered the specimen with the souvenir recorded on the report. Both use every attempt as the denominator. **Seconds** is the median successful ordinary room time from the start of the hand’s plan to extraction, excluding the model's initial observation and the final animation. These are route-aware simulations. The pointer recording checks the capacitor separately.

Fourteen routes started below the 40% take-home target, including Field Check. Every route now reaches at least 45% at 20 seeds. Nigel's skylight and Tom's smoke route finish at 45% because later hazards can still end the attempt. The local change column identifies placement and layout edits. Other rows use the shared pickup fixes.
