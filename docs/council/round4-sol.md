## #0003 · Baker Mabel’s First Batch

1. **Humour · First-clear cover story.** “Customers praise the best service in years” repeats the judgment the paw-printed OPEN sign and Vrell’s “Cash only” already let the player make. Give the cover row the funniest consequence of running a bakery while its baker is missing. Keep the sign and Vrell’s first-clear line. [First-clear report](/home/stone/dev/night-shift/docs/GDD-11-onramp.md:138)

   - EN “The first loaf clocked in. Mabel is still listed as out on delivery.”
   - FR « Le premier pain a pointé. Mabel est toujours notée “en livraison”. »

2. **Humour · French Gazette.** “Montent au septième ciel” takes the headline from a saucer witness account to a familiar euphemism. The double meaning is already in *miches*. Let the sighting provide the verb. [Gazette](/home/stone/dev/night-shift/docs/GDD-11-onramp.md:132)

   - EN **BAKER’S BUNS SEEN RISING INTO NIGHT SKY**
   - FR **LES MICHES DE MABEL S’ENVOLENT EN PLEINE NUIT**

The photocopied slipper silhouette and Larry’s unclaimed breakfast roll are enough. Crumb, the motion light and the pie still read on a first run that goes straight to the roof.

Keep Gary’s affiliation and the four catches. Let players find Nora’s checklist in the room. ## #0012 · 1122 · Ni!

1. **Ideas · The rabbit’s meaningful choice is easy to miss.** The fixed sentry catches the first beeline, but the rabbit arms only below hover speed and the spec tells players to cruise past. The quoted panic bark costs a full sighting, even when the rabbit does something funny that the player escapes. Give the inner crossing a visible reason to pass within reach and play the settled bark when the lunge commits. That lets an observant player get the joke and recover. Keep the east-bank bypass. [Inner crossing and rabbit](/home/stone/dev/night-shift/docs/GDD-12-1122-grail.md:39) · [Rabbit barks](/home/stone/dev/night-shift/docs/GDD-12-1122-grail.md:121) · [Monty Python’s film notes](https://montypython.com/film_Monty%2BPython%2Band%2Bthe%2BHoly%2BGrail%2B%281975%29/15)

   - EN spec “Mark the inner bridge-to-tower shortcut with cart ruts through the cave’s lunge half-disc. Put the wait notch outside the cow marks and rabbit footprint. Play ‘Run away! Run away!’ once on the first committed lunge.”
   - FR spec « Marquer le raccourci du pont à la tour par des ornières qui traversent le demi-disque d’attaque. Placer la zone d’attente hors des points de chute et de portée du lapin. Jouer “Fuyez ! Fuyez !” une fois, au premier bond. »

2. **Callbacks · The shrubbery’s found status.** “Nine hundred years … in this exact spot” asks players to reconcile a harbour-side hedge with a house on Birch Lane. The new clipped hedge is enough to make the visual rhyme. Keep the Hendersons and the knights’ second demand in a line that also works for a returning player who skipped Birch Lane. [Shrubbery status](/home/stone/dev/night-shift/docs/GDD-12-1122-grail.md:85) · [Birch Lane hedge](/home/stone/dev/night-shift/docs/GDD-11-onramp.md:172)

   - EN “The knights would like it a little higher. Judging by the Hendersons’ hedge, someone took notes.”
   - FR « Les chevaliers le voudraient un peu plus haut. À voir la haie des Henderson, quelqu’un a pris des notes. »

3. **Humour · Memo sign-off.** The expense for half a coconut and Zib asking to expense the other half are the same punchline across two entry screens. Keep the launch line, where the horse substitution is in view. Give Vrell a separate limit in the memo. [Memo sign-off](/home/stone/dev/night-shift/docs/GDD-12-1122-grail.md:19) · [Launch](/home/stone/dev/night-shift/docs/GDD-12-1122-grail.md:117)

   - EN “— Vrell. No jousting on Bureau time.”
   - FR « — Vrell. Pas de joute pendant les heures de service. »

The coconut patrol, cow landing, rabbit silhouette, TOO HIGH board and per-bonk armour make the film legible at a glance. “Fuyez ! Fuyez !” works as local copy. The catalogue confirms a French-dubbed edition. The dub’s wording remains unverified. [French edition catalogue](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb444600615)

## #0013 · 1953 · The War of the Colds

1. **Humour and callbacks · Vrell’s first clear.** “Borrowed … from behind” still reads as the manner in which Vrell borrowed the thermometer. “Humans made it up” leaves the object of the lie buried in an earlier level. Give the temperature-taking a verb and put the audience’s mistaken interpretation in the same line. The joke then works cold and answers the L6 memo. Preserve the arbitration’s ingredients by giving Compliance the cover row. The new comments are 120 characters in EN and 122 in FR, within the 135-character target. [First-clear report](/home/stone/dev/night-shift/docs/GDD-13-1953-tripods.md:131) · [Live L6 memo](/home/stone/dev/night-shift/src/i18n/en.ts:476)

   - EN first clear “Grandpa Doyle lent me his brass thermometer. I took temperatures from behind. The whole drive-in thought it was a probe.”
   - FR first clear « Papy Doyle m’a prêté son thermomètre en laiton. J’ai pris les températures par derrière. Le drive-in a cru voir une sonde. »
   - EN cover “Fever log filed as Form 7-B. Compliance insists it was a special effect.”
   - FR cover « Relevé classé formulaire 7-B. La Conformité maintient que c’était un trucage. »

2. **Ideas · Heat-ray shortcut.** The round-3 fix gets the nominal crossing over the fuse. The new explicit Hush Drive requirement has only 17 ms of path headroom and 33 ms of burst headroom on that line. A small cut across the bend or a late first sample can erase the catch. Give the full-speed, fully live test a margin. A range of 640 px gives about 474 px of coverage on the marked centreline, or 1.10 s at the cap. The wait-pocket centre at x = 300 has about 55 px of radial clearance. Keep the exact collision shape visible and prove a normal clear-window crossing in the greybox. [Sneeze exposure](/home/stone/dev/night-shift/docs/GDD-13-1953-tripods.md:57) · [Live fuse update](/home/stone/dev/night-shift/src/flight/deep-sim.ts:129)

   - EN spec “Start the northwest ray at 640 px and 1.22 s live. Move the wait pocket to (300, 1300) and keep the bend through (800, 1300). Check live catches and clear crossings across the usable lane, with and without Hush Drive.”
   - FR spec « Régler le rayon nord-ouest à 640 px et 1,22 s d’émission. Déplacer la zone d’attente en (300, 1300) et garder le virage par (800, 1300). Vérifier la détection et le passage pendant l’accalmie sur toute la largeur utile, avec et sans le Moteur Feutré. »

3. **Callbacks · Present-day coop and repeat pickup.** The same leg silhouette is in L1 after the player files the leg in 1953, and the repeat report says it is back on the tripod. The wooden block already placed under the coop in 1953 supplies a cleaner visual link. Age it for L1 and show the old three-toed imprint beside it. The 1953 leg can then stay a find from Grandpa Doyle’s farm. [1953 pickup and L1 scenery](/home/stone/dev/night-shift/docs/GDD-13-1953-tripods.md:86) · [Repeat pickup](/home/stone/dev/night-shift/docs/GDD-13-1953-tripods.md:139)

   - EN scenery direction “On present-day Doyle Farm, the coop rests on a weathered timber block. Beside it, a shallow three-toed print shows what used to hold it up.”
   - FR scenery direction « À la ferme Doyle, de nos jours, le poulailler repose sur une cale de bois usée. À côté, une empreinte à trois doigts trahit l’ancien support. »
   - EN repeat “The leg goes in the cabinet. The Division asked if we could lend it a broom handle.”
   - FR repeat « La patte va en vitrine. La division demande si on peut lui prêter un manche à balai. »

The copper machine against the drive-in screen owns the film connection. The probe eye, unthreading cylinder, church refuge and last reel give the rest of the shift its own 1953 references. [AFI catalogue](https://catalog.afi.com/Film/51100-THE-WAR-OF-THE-WORLDS)

## #0014 · 2199 · Your Town Is in the Way

1. **Ideas · The east dozer fails its timing job at the specified ring.** With the current route and the steward’s 18 px lamp offset, the Ticket loft centre is exposed for about **0.17 s** per loop, during the north turn. That is shorter than the **0.80 s** fuse even if the player parks on the centre. From its designated north pocket the centreline is essentially a free approach. Put the patrol on a street north of the ticket office so the player can wait out a southbound pass. Sampling the same cone and turn with the route below gives **1.34 s** at the ring centre and about **6.22 s** clear. The lower connector, Service door centre and moved wait-pocket centre stay clear in the geometric check. Prove the full hover area and escape path in the greybox. [Dozer and fast rings](/home/stone/dev/night-shift/docs/GDD-14-2199-hitchhiker.md:41) · [Steward turn and cone](/home/stone/dev/night-shift/src/flight/photographer.ts:129)

   - EN spec “Move the dozer to (1270, 400) ↔ (1270, 600), with the Ticket loft wait pocket at (1490, 560). Keep its speed, dwell and cone. Mark the approach leading southwest from the turnout and show where the southbound lamp crosses it.”
   - FR spec « Déplacer le bulldozer sur (1270, 400) ↔ (1270, 600) et placer la zone d’attente de la mezzanine en (1490, 560). Garder la vitesse, le temps de demi-tour et le cône. Tracer l’approche vers le sud-ouest depuis le refuge et montrer où le phare la balaie en descendant vers le sud. »

2. **Callbacks · The last bill in panel 1.** The script says Krix gets the bill, but the panel’s two readable marks are ORDER VOID and RECEIVED. A player can see Krix signing for the voided order and miss the fact that he owes the demolition costs. Label the sheet he receives so the season’s corporate gag plays in the image, with the toaster pop on its own beat. [Finale panel 1](/home/stone/dev/night-shift/docs/GDD-14-2199-hitchhiker.md:183)

   - EN bill heading **DEMOLITION CHARGES · BILL TO KRIX**
   - FR bill heading **FRAIS DE DÉMOLITION · À PAYER PAR KRIX**

3. **References · Ear fish in the trophy catalogue.** A fish translating a written tax return could be any science-fiction translator. The ear and spoken language make the allusion immediately recognisable. Keep the jar and the original name. Give the trophy one joke about what the fish hears. [Ear fish catalogue](/home/stone/dev/night-shift/docs/GDD-14-2199-hitchhiker.md:91) · [Douglas Adams’s script, publisher’s extract](https://extracts.panmacmillan.com/extract?isbn=9781529048476)

   - EN “Fits in one ear. Zib tried it at a recital. It translated three stanzas as ‘Move your car.’”
   - FR « Se glisse dans l’oreille. Zib l’a essayé pendant un récital. Trois strophes traduites par “Déplacez votre véhicule.” »

4. **Humour · Dozer sighting.** “I’m not lying down in front of anything” describes Zib’s refusal and comes after the lamp has already made them flee. Use the dressing gown as the absurd safety equipment they forgot. [Dozer sighting](/home/stone/dev/night-shift/docs/GDD-14-2199-hitchhiker.md:164) · [Disney’s film synopsis](https://d23.com/a-to-z/hitchhikers-guide-to-the-galaxy/)
