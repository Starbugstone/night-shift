## #0003 · Baker Mabel's First Batch

1. **The pie is on a back step, but the cartoon pie cools on a windowsill** (References). "Cartoon law" only lands if
   the picture matches. Keep the coordinates and draw a short outbuilding wall with a sill under the pie.
   - Memo: "There's a cherry pie cooling on the back windowsill. Cartoon law says that makes it fair game."
   - Unfound hint: "Cooling on the bakery's back windowsill."
2. **Mabel's mumble "Charge extra. Call it crust…" is unclear** (Humour). Replace with: "It's not burnt… it's artisan…"
3. **Zib's retry line "I can still see my skeleton" needs decoding** (Humour). Replace with: "Around the lamp this
   time. It knows what I look like now."
4. **"Crumb opened on time" has no picture** (Ideas). Add a cover-story row on first clear: "Cover story: the cat runs
   the bakery now. Nobody has questioned it." In the report art, show the OPEN sign with a floury paw print.
5. **Pie catalogue text is the weakest of the three items** (Humour). Replace with: "Cherry pie. Left out to cool,
   unattended, at night. The Bureau calls that consent. Legal calls it a grey area."
6. **Too many callbacks for a one-specimen level** (Callbacks). Larry's roll, the slipper flyer, the odd sock and
   cats-as-management make four; the rule is one or two. Cut the odd sock under Crumb's paw, since the handoff line
   already has forty socks.

## #0004 · While the Hendersons Are Away

1. **Three "since <year>" jokes in one level** (Humour, the "a year older" problem). Pruitt 1994, Len 1998 and the
   gnome 1986 share a template. Keep Pruitt's and change the other two.
   - Len: "Has borrowed the Hendersons' tools for so long he calls it 'storage'. They're too polite to ask."
   - Gnome, collected on a repeat: "He's requested a window seat. He's faced the same drive his whole life."
2. **"Their garden gnome" in the memo has no clear owner** (Humour). It reads as Pruitt's. Replace with: "The
   Hendersons' garden gnome is sulking by Len's east drive."
3. **Zib's return line "One aboard. Sun's still coming up. Next house." has no joke** (Humour). This is the first
   two-specimen shift, so use that: "One aboard. That's already my record. Next house."
4. **Card line "Two sleepers. One nosy neighbour." is flat** (Humour). Replace with: "Two sleepers. One neighbour who
   isn't."
5. **The bin-lid decoy saucer has no payoff** (Ideas). Add one Zib bark on first approach: "Is that one of ours? …It's
   a bin lid. Nice lines, though."
6. **Len's three-delivery line is clunky** (Humour). Replace with: "Fixed the squeak in the hangar door. The squeak was
   the doorbell."
7. **The 2199 postcard can tie to the shrubbery** (Callbacks). "Home. The Hendersons didn't notice." becomes "Home. The
   hedge is enormous. Still no Hendersons." It still works cold.
8. **Len's mumble "Tea, milk, no chain oil…" is thin** (Humour). Replace with: "It's not the wheel ticking… it's me…"

## 1122 · Ni!

1. **The most quotable on-topic line is missing** (References, Humour). The French "already got one" is a ready-made
   census joke. Add it to the sentry rotation after the first sighting: "A census? We've already got one!"
2. **Zib's Mum has a free callback in the taunts** (Callbacks). Third line in the rotation: sentry "Your mother was a
   hamster!", Zib "Leave Mum out of this. She has Head Office on speed dial." It warms up the 2199 payoff and needs no
   setup.
3. **The TOO HIGH board contradicts "they want a slightly taller one"** (Humour). Keep the board and move the repeat
   line to the film's next demand: "You brought a shrubbery. Now they want a tree cut down with a herring. I'm hanging
   up."
4. **Holy Hand Grenade text says "Pin in" and then it explodes** (Humour). Replace with: "Count to three. Not four. Zib
   counted to five. The hold has a skylight now."
5. **Enchanter's horns text is unclear** (Humour). Replace with: "Ram's horns on a skullcap. The ram has been asking
   for them back."
6. **Black Knight's arm text misses the obvious joke** (Humour). Replace with: "A spare. He keeps four on the peg. He
   gets through them."
7. **Rodney's ten-delivery line ignores his minstrels** (References). Replace with: "Hired a minstrel to sing of his
   courage. The song has nine verses. He runs away in eight."
8. **Tom's second mumble "Next spell… indoors voice…" is weak** (Humour). Tie it to his sleep excuse: "Just the one
   candle… stand back… further…"
9. **Missed-pickup line is vague** (Humour). Replace with: "You left the shrubbery. They've been saying Ni down the
   phone since midnight. I've put them through to Accounting."
10. **One art-only visual gag is missing** (Ideas). Park an abandoned wooden rabbit on wheels outside the French
    outwork, with no collider, away from the cave so it isn't read as the hazard.

## 1953 · The War of the Worlds

1. **The title is just the film's name** (Humour, References). Every other era title is a joke. The cold is the one
   thing everyone remembers, and 1953 gives a second meaning: **"The War of the Colds"** / « La Guerre des rhumes ».
2. **The thermometer payoff has two holes** (Callbacks, continuity).
   - "That's where humans got the probe idea" never says who saw it.
   - The shipped L6 memo has Vrell saying "We have no idea where humans got their other theories".
   - One line fixes both: "I borrowed Grandpa Doyle's brass thermometer. Forty temperatures, none by mouth, in front of
     a full drive-in. That's where humans got the idea. I told Compliance we had no clue. Keep it that way."
   - That is about 190 characters against a 135 budget, so check wrapping. If it must shrink, cut "in front of a full
     drive-in" last, because it is the fix.
3. **Zib's launch line has no joke** (Humour). Replace with: "Our tripod's blocking the screen and they're applauding.
   They think it's 3-D." Give the front rows cardboard 3-D glasses.
4. **The rooms have no character gag like 1122's** (Ideas). All three below are cosmetic and reuse existing parts.
   - Pastor Matt drops one biscuit per registered bonk, using the Black Knight's shed pool, with mumbles "That was the
     shortbread…" then "Those were for the Martians…".
   - Sylvie's free arm points to the exit while she is carried. It points the wrong way on the short route.
   - Clive's Geiger counter ticks faster as his wake meter rises, on top of the existing tell.
5. **Tripod-leg cover story is flat** (Humour). Vrell's cover stories are the swamp-gas voice: "It was a weather
   balloon. Weather balloons have knees now."
6. **Martian lens text is clunky** (Humour). Replace with: "Red, green and blue. The Martians saw Grimsby in full
   colour and came anyway."
7. **Pickup repeat line trips on "left" for a three-legged machine** (Humour). Replace with: "Another leg. The parts
   catalogue lists left, right and 'other'. This is the other."
8. **Sylvie has one mumble; the others have two** (Humour). Add: "Row by row… not you, sir, you're on fire…"
9. **The sick tripod looks no sicker than the healthy ones** (Ideas). Put an ice pack on its hood and a trailing
   scarf. Don't give it a red nose, because red means "it has you".
10. **French "ciné-parc" is Québec usage.** France says "drive-in".

## 2199 · Your Town Is in the Way

1. **Penny's three archive lines are the same hitchhiking joke** (Humour). Keep the driving test and make the other two
   about her guide.
   - Three deliveries: "Reviewed the cargo hold. Two stars. 'Roomy. The other guests were unconscious.'"
   - Ten deliveries: "Updated her guide. Earth now reads 'Mostly harmless. Grimsby: less so.'" This adds the one famous
     phrase the level lacks.
2. **One couplet will wear out on replay** (Humour). The opening reciter needs a rotation of three; keep the current
   verse first.
   - "I think that I shall never see / a town I can't reduce to scree."
   - "Roses are red, your roof is slate. / Kindly vacate by half past eight."
3. **The towel's best-known line is unused** (References). Unfound hint: "Always know where your towel is. Yours is on
   the west harbour rack."
4. **Dozer sighting line is a 7** (Humour). Replace with: "The bulldozer saw me. It's filed me under 'obstacle,
   small'."
5. **Bubbles' "Nobody taught me." won't read cold** (Callbacks). Replace with: "P.S. Nobody coached me. I read your
   files."
6. **Penny's second mumble "blink twice" is weak** (Humour). Replace with: "Any planet's fine… not this one…"
7. **Spare dressing gown text is muddled** (Humour). Replace with: "Artie's formal one. Identical to the other, but
   he's ironed the belt."
8. **"Eleven" is now used three times in the game.** Gary's laser count, Vrell's pension weeks and Moe's pages all say
   eleven. Change Moe's to: "He's on page four. Gary's footnotes start on page five."
9. **Poetry needs a pilot reaction** (Ideas). While the saucer is inside an amber preview edge, Zib sticks fingers in
   their ears in the cockpit. Cosmetic only.
