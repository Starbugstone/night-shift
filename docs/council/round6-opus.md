## #0012 · 1122 Ni! (Humour 9)

1. **“Say Ni to X” appears twice.** The pickup cover story “Knights say Ni to garden centre price hike” and the missed-rotation line
   “The knights are saying Ni to Accounting. Accounting is saying it back” are the same pun. A player who plays once with the
   shrubbery and once without sees both. Keep the Accounting line, which has the better tag. Replace the cover story with the town’s
   explanation of what the player actually caused: the “Ni!” pill stops once the shrubbery is taken (GDD-12:83).
   - EN: “Knights finally quiet. Neighbours suspect a shrubbery.”
   - FR: « Les chevaliers se taisent enfin. Les voisins soupçonnent un jardinet. »

## #0014 · 2199 Your Town Is in the Way (Humour 9)

1. **The new Gazette is the third line about catching a lift on a saucer.** Penny’s `at3` (“Hitched a lift on our saucer”), Penny’s
   `at10` (“Thumbs a lift off every saucer that passes”) and now the Gazette (“WITNESSES WOULD NOW LIKE A LIFT”) all tell the same
   joke. In French all three use *faire du stop* word for word. The Gazette is the strongest of the three on a demolition loss, so

   - EN `at3`: “Reviewed our saucer. Two stars. Docked one for the probe.”
