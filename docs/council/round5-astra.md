I’d ship the bakery, the Hendersons and the drive-in as written. Three small changes would bring the other two to the same point. These scores cover the current design scripts. The staging findings use the specified placements and the existing portrait camera.

## #0003 · Baker Mabel’s First Batch

The player lifts Mabel and discovers she’s left a floury outline on the bench. That’s a laugh the player gets to cause. On the stockroom route, the next thing to watch is her feet dangling over the cat with the keys. The paw print on OPEN earns “Cash only.” Larry’s unclaimed breakfast roll makes sense to someone who met him one shift ago and tells us Mabel still bakes for him. I’d keep all four beats.

## #0004 · While the Hendersons Are Away

Gary holding the installer’s manual in his fourth alarm photo is the detail that earns the count. The player can see exactly how competent he is on either garage route. Pruitt turning a binder page gives the player a reason to watch her hands. “Two sleepers. One neighbour who isn’t” tells the whole story in six words. The gnome’s “Wet. Cows. Wish you were here” is worth finding on the shelf after 1122.

## #0012 · Ni! (1122)

**Humour 9 · Give the coconut opener its picture.** Zib says “They don’t have horses. Just coconuts” at the launch fork. The squire’s nearest patrol point is 1,230 logical pixels north of launch. On a 960-pixel-high screen, a player hears the explanation well before seeing a single coconut. Keep the settled launch line and dress the fork with one prop using the existing coconut art. [Launch and patrol](../GDD-12-1122-grail.md#flight)

- **EN staging** — “At the launch fork, hang a pair of coconut halves from a horse’s reins on an empty hitching rail. Frame the shells beside Zib when the launch line plays.”
- **FR mise en scène** — « À la bifurcation de départ, deux demi-noix de coco pendent au bout de rênes attachées à une barre d’attache vide. Cadrez les coques à côté de Zib au moment de sa réplique. »

The Black Knight needs no such help. The player makes the dent, sees the armour come off and hears him insist he’s fine. The toaster getting two minutes on the witness stand gives Krix a payoff even for a player who never collected him.

## #0013 · The War of the Colds (1953)

The drive-in audience can now see what it later mistakes for a probe. On the east approach, the player can spot the patients guarding their rear hatches and recognise young Vrell by his clipboard. The first clear supplies the missing action in plain language in both versions. It even works for a player who took West Farm Road and missed the queue. “Bless you!” gives the heat ray its own laugh during play, leaving the thermometer reveal for Vrell. I’d ship it.

## #0014 · Your Town Is in the Way (2199)

1. **Humour 9 · Zib sees the book before the player can.** “He’s opened a book” plays at the fork. The normal launch camera starts at world y = 1,360. The opening guard is centred at y = 1,350, putting even the visible sliver of the guard at the very top of the screen. The listener slumping farther down is a good visible gag. Give Zib a first-leg line that a player can understand there, including on the safe route. The first recital can then answer it. [Opening placements](../GDD-14-2199-hitchhiker.md#poetry-guards-and-the-demolition-dozer) · [Camera placement](../../src/flight/deep-sim.ts#L306)

   - **EN, Zib at the fork** — “They’ve sent poets. Vrell, are their books loaded?”
   - **FR, Zib au départ** — « Ils ont envoyé des poètes. Vrell, leurs livres sont chargés ? »

2. **Humour 9 · The Gazette repeats Vrell on a demolition loss.** Vrell says “We’ve been invited to comment on the rubble.” After two sightings the clipping reads “RUBBLE ENTERS PUBLIC CONSULTATION.” The report can show both. Vrell already has the better version of the planning-office joke. Let the paper report what the eyewitnesses want from the passing saucer. [Deadline and Gazette copy](../GDD-14-2199-hitchhiker.md#copy) · [Report comment and headline selection](../../src/ui/report.ts#L129)

   - **EN, Gazette** — **WITNESSES SAW SAUCER. WITNESSES WOULD NOW LIKE A LIFT.**
   - **FR, Gazette** — **DES TÉMOINS ONT VU LA SOUCOUPE. ILS AIMERAIENT QU’ELLE LES PRENNE EN STOP.**

The ending earns its four callback beats. Krix gets a bill he would prefer to leave unsigned. Bubbles gets to say goodbye in her own handwriting. Vrell puts his retirement in LATER. Zib gets the last word about the pool. Each character gets something different out of the same successful shift.
