# Council review, round 1 · Fable (comedy and visual gags)

Read-only review of the five planned levels in `docs/GDD-11-onramp.md`, `GDD-12-1122-grail.md`,
`GDD-13-1953-tripods.md` and `GDD-14-2199-hitchhiker.md`, done on `preprod` on 2026-11-25. Checked against
`docs/GDD-6-story.md`, `docs/GDD-10-1955.md`, `docs/story/continuity-review.md`, `docs/story/decisions.md`
(D1–D7) and the live copy in `src/i18n/en.ts`. No game file was changed.

Scores are demanding: a 7 means "fine", not shippable. A 10 means ship as is and a player would quote it.
Nothing is marked down for era order, for having few callbacks, or for points already settled by D1–D7.

The Grail level and 2199 have the right skeleton and need line work. 1953 is the weak one: its central joke
(Martians with colds) is never something the player dodges, sees or laughs at in play.

## Era order

Highlight **1122 as the next dispatch after 1955, then 1953, then 2199**, with 1122 and 1953 both open as D7 says.

- **Variety:** 1953 straight after 1955 is two 1950s small-town levels in a row on the same palette.
  Lightning-and-clock-tower into cows-and-coconuts is the bigger laugh.
- **Difficulty:** the docs' own casual Ghost targets already ramp this way (40–45%, then 35–40%, then 33–38%).
- **Story:** the thermometer payoff lands later and closer to the finale. 2199's "Vrell recognises Krix's docket
  from 1122" holds in either order because 2199 needs both.

Because either order is allowed, three things must work both ways:

- **1122 first clear** names the next date. Replace with: "The witnesses are in. Nobody owns the cup. Krix's
  lawyers are billing him for the cows."
- **The Tripod sick-leave tease in 1122** must read as a setup or as a callback. Use a dawn-fail line instead:
  "Dawn. Still better staffed than the Tripod Division."
- **The 2199 return ping** should fire on whichever of the two is delivered second, not on the 1953 clear.

## #0003 Baker Mabel's First Batch

The copy is pleasant but nobody will quote it, and a bakery at PG-13 with no bun joke is a waste.

1. **Pickup: the bent bread tray is a grey rectangle.** It won't read at phone size and the "pouring corner" joke
   is thin. Replace it with an **uncollected wedding cake** on the loading apron (white tiers read instantly).
   - Memo hint: "There's an uncollected wedding cake on the west loading apron. The wedding was in March."
   - Catalogue: "Uncollected wedding cake. 'Congratulations Dean & Sharon.' Dean's name has been re-iced twice."
   - Found status: "Top tier missing. Zib denies everything. Zib has icing on their chin."
   - Left behind: "Leave the cake. It's waited since March. It's good at it."
2. **Mabel's mumble has no joke.** Replace "The dough's resting. I'm supervising…" with "Hands off my buns… they
   haven't risen…"
3. **Gazette is limp.** Replace with **BAKER VANISHES. CAT DECLARES BAKERY "UNDER NEW MANAGEMENT"**, which pays
   the cats-are-management joke.
4. **Vrell's first delivery line.** Replace with "Mabel's aboard. Best-smelling specimen we've ever filed. Don't
   tell Larry."
5. **Souvenir: the dough scraper is dull.** Swap it for **yesterday's baguette**: "Hard enough to count as a
   weapon in four systems. Zib has knighted themselves with it twice."
6. **Visual gags (cheap):** Crumb kneads the air in its sleep ("making biscuits"). Each bonk puffs flour off
   Mabel, and she leaves a floury outline on the bench.
7. **Continuity (settled by the decisions):** "your first paid call" and "two small paid jobs" contradict Zib
   being unpaid until L8. Use "your first live call".
8. **Keep:** "poked the saucer wall and said it needed another ten minutes" is the best line in the doc. The memo
   joke about the cat being in charge also stays.

**Callbacks.** Crumb already carries cats-are-management.

- Add Doyle's missing-slipper flyer in the bakery window, with no text needed.
- The "Don't tell Larry" line in item 4 is the second one.
- **New small arc**, since this level has little history to call on: the uncollected wedding cake for Dean &
  Sharon. Pay it off in a Maple Court Gazette line (**WEDDING POSTPONED AGAIN. CAKE "ABDUCTED", SAYS GROOM**) and
  once more in 2199 (see Moe's at3 below).

## #0004 The Neighbours Are Asleep

The stepladder/laser joke, the checklist and the bin lid are good. Len is underwritten and two of Zib's lines are
pure instructions.

1. **Title.** "While the Hendersons Are Away" uses the running scapegoats; the current title is flat.
2. **Memo is about 110 words against a 180–230 character body budget, and uses Mrs. Pruitt and Gary before they
   are introduced** (the decisions say to introduce them cleanly here). Replacement:
   - Body: "Bring back Nora Bell, house-sitting at the Hendersons', and Len Briggs from the bike workshop next
     door, before dawn. Mrs. Pruitt across the lane is up with binoculars: when they glint, wait, then cross. A
     runaway bin lid sits by Len's east drive."
   - Joke: "Nora asked the neighbourhood watch for a stepladder. Gary lent her a laser. Wait for the beam to drop,
     then mind her feet."
   - Keep the sign-off "— Vrell, who keeps a perfectly good stepladder".
3. **Bin lid: the obvious joke is missed.** It's a flying disc. New catalogue: "Runaway bin lid. Mrs. Pruitt has
   reported it as a UFO eleven times. Tonight she was finally right, about the wrong object."
4. **Len's mumble isn't a joke.** Replace "Mind the back wheel. It remembers…" with "Tick… tick… it's not the
   wheel… it's never the wheel…"
5. **Zib's lines.**
   - Launch: "Cross when she looks away. Does she ever look away?"
   - Return to flight: "One aboard! I'm officially half done. Next ring."
6. **Both of Len's routes give the same bell.** Give the skylight a **biscuit tin**: "Contains bolts. The tin
   labelled BOLTS contains biscuits. Len has never been wrong twice." (47 of 48 shelf slots.)
7. **Fern count.** It appears four times (checklist, description, at3, mumble). Drop it from Nora's description:
   "Keeps a list of keys, and a second list of where she put the first list. Lost the second list."
8. **Visual gags:** Len asleep with his ear on a wheel that's still slowly ticking round. Nora asleep holding a
   dripping watering can.
9. **Keep:** the house-sitting checklist, the magnetic parts dish, the bicycle bell, "extension without planning
   permission" and **LOW-FLYING SAUCER FAILS TO INDICATE, SAYS MRS. PRUITT**. The Hendersons staying off screen
   is right.

**Callbacks.** Pruitt and Gary are set up here, so the level mostly looks forward. One look back is enough.

- Add to Nora's checklist: "Collect the Hendersons' bread order from Mabel's. (Mabel's is shut. Nobody knows
  why.)" It shows the last shift had consequences.

## 1122 Ni!

This is the best design of the five: cows, coconuts, the rabbit and the fleeing knight are all mechanics that
are the joke.

1. **Other films in the archive (D4).** Rodney's "Beam me out, Scotty" (already used by Sterling in live copy)
   and the Knight's lightsaber break "everything comes from the film".
   - Rodney at10: "Has bravely run away from the weigh-in, the biscuit and the exit interview. His minstrel has
     been asked to stop singing about it."
   - Black Knight at10: "Lost an arm-wrestle to the cargo door. Demanded best of three. Then best of five. We're
     calling it a draw."
2. **The Black Knight gag has no escalation.** Tie his mumbles to the bonk count, in order, and bend them as L9
   did:
   - "'Tis but a nap."
   - "I've had worse."
   - "Just a flesh snore."
   - "Right. We'll call it a draw."
   - Report line when he arrives stripped: "Delivered: one knight. Some assembly required."
3. **The French taunter has no taunt.** It's the most quoted scene and the sentry is the main beeline stop.
   Sighting causes:
   - "The sentry spots you: 'Your saucer is a hamster and your supervisor smells of elderberries!'"
   - "Spotted. He farts in your general airspace."
4. **Tom loses the name joke.** Open his description with "There are some who call him… Tom." then keep the
   match line.
5. **Holy Hand Grenade.** Replace the monk line with "Instructions: count to three. Zib went 'one, two, five'.
   Vrell has taken it away."
6. **Zib's launch line is unclear.** Replace "Is the horse supposed to fit in his pockets?" with "No horse. Just
   a man banging coconuts. And everyone's fine with this?"
7. **The rabbit needs its beat.** First approach: "Aw, a bunny!" Sighting cause: "That was NOT a bunny."
8. **Enchanter's horns are a weak souvenir.** Swap for a **nibbled helmet**: "One bite mark. The rabbit sends its
   regards."
9. **Missing gag that fits this game exactly.** A parked cart on the west cart track with one speech pill,
   "Bring out your sleepers!", and a wake-fail line, "'I'm not dead!' Correct. You're census."
10. **Rodney's second mumble.** Replace "Retreat in my sleep…" with "Bravely… bravely napping…"
11. **Verbatim quotes.** "Fetchez la vache!", "'Tis but a scratch" and "Run away!" are lifted exactly; GDD-6 and
    L9 say bend, don't quote. Items 2 and 10 cover two of them. Make the cow cue "Fetchez la moo!"
12. **First clear and the sick-leave tease.** See "Era order": "The witnesses are in. Nobody owns the cup. Krix's
    lawyers are billing him for the cows." and the dawn-fail line "Dawn. Still better staffed than the Tripod
    Division."
13. **Keep:** the memo, "Three witnesses. The cows are hostile.", the coconut and swallow catalogue lines, Tom's
    ordinary-rabbit spell, **KNIGHTS SAY NI TO GARDEN CENTRE PRICE HIKE** and the "slightly taller one" pickup line.

**Callbacks.** Krix leads, which is right, but nothing else ties the keep to Grimsby.

- Shrubbery status: "In nine hundred years this will be the Hendersons' hedge. It is already being blamed for
  things."
- Dawn-fail line, which is also the film's ending: "Dawn. A police car pulls up and arrests the entire century.
  Unit 4 says hello."
- Krix's witness roll is stamped with a small toaster seal, with no text.

**Bold change.** The Knight's escalating mumbles (item 2) are the one to build first: they turn every bonk in his
room into the film's best scene.

## 1953 The War of the Worlds

1. **The cold is never a hazard.** Replace the reused press flashbulb with a **sneezing tripod**, using the same
   long-exposure rectangle system. Amber build-up "ah… ah… AH…", then the heat ray fires across the frame on
   "CHOO". The crowd adds a "Bless you!" pill. This is the change that moves the level most.
2. **The doc ties itself in knots over legs.** The 1953 film's machines hover, so the tripod leg becomes a
   "removable landing strut". Players picture tripods, and live copy already has a Tripod Division. Draw real
   tripods with the film's cobra-neck hood and three-colour eye. Leg status: "The Division now fields one bipod.
   It leans on things."
3. **The thermometer origin has no punchline.** This is the game's biggest probe joke and it explains why the
   Division is still on sick leave. The decisions say to pay it off once, at the first clear. Vrell, first clear:
   "Forty pilots, one thermometer, and only one way to take a Martian's temperature. They've been 'on sick leave'
   ever since. Wouldn't you?"
4. **The memo spends that joke early and says the Bureau invaded.** Replacement second paragraph: "It wasn't an
   invasion. The Tripod Division just parks like one. I was there that night. Don't ask about the thermometer."
5. **Title is just the film's title.** Use "The Cold War of the Worlds".
6. **Three other films in the archive (D4 allows one at most).** Close Encounters, Men in Black and E.T. are
   stacked in, and the E.T. finger repeats Pemberton's live line.
   - Clive at10: "Has explained the invasion to four generals using a blackboard and the word 'gentlemen'."
   - Sylvie at10: "Still stamps NO REFUNDS on anything that hovers. The saucer has been stamped twice."
   - Matt at10: "Still leaves the extra chair out. It has been sat in once. He won't say by what."
7. **Projector sightings need the drive-in joke.** "Caught in the projector beam. The audience boos: 'You can see
   the strings!'" and "Spotted. Somebody yells 'Down in front!'"
8. **Sleepers are named after the film's characters, which nobody remembers.** Make them B-movie types instead.
   Sylvie becomes the ticket girl who screamed herself hoarse and silently mouths "AAAA" in her sleep. Clive
   gets a pipe and "In English, Professor…" as a mumble.
9. **Zero-reader gag:** a queue of tripods behind the screen in scarves with hot-water bottles, and rookie Vrell
   as a small clipboard-holding silhouette at the head of it. Zib sees him; present-day Vrell never does, so the
   "never meets himself" rule holds.
10. **Weak souvenir lines.**
    - Lens: "Sees in three colours. All three looked at Grimsby and asked to go home."
    - Clapper: "Rang all night. The Martians thought it was last orders."
11. **Keep:** both Gazette headlines (**MARTIANS INVADE. MARTIANS CATCH COLD. MARTIANS GO HOME.** and **DRIVE-IN
    AUDIENCE DEMANDS LESS REALISTIC SEQUEL**), the programme refund line, the reel and truce-flag lines, and
    "Tripod Division claims a limp" if the bipod line isn't taken.

**Callbacks.** The strongest story ties already (thermometer, Form 7-B, Tripod Division).

- Make the farm Doyle's father's: "I borrowed the thermometer from a farmer named Doyle. It was for cattle. I
  didn't ask. Neither did the pilots." This sharpens the probe joke as well. Use it in the first clear or the
  thermometer's catalogue text, not both.
- A small girl in the back seat of one drive-in car with toy binoculars and a notebook, with no text.

## 2199 Your Town Is in the Way

Poetry guards with a fainting listener and the dolphin's exit are exactly right. The souvenirs and archive
under-use the film, and the finale's last line is flat.

1. **Final panel.** "Your own parking ring, Zib. She signed it over." isn't a closer. It should pay "adequate"
   and the pool tip ("Hers comes with a pool"), with D6's pension beat. Replacement: Vrell slides his retirement
   form into a drawer marked LATER, then says "Your own parking ring, Zib. It's adequate." Zib: "It has a POOL."
   The ring is drawn with Bubbles' pool still in it and the saucer floating.
2. **Vrell's first clear needs weight and D5.** "Three residents. Grimsby stays, Krix gets the invoice. Your
   mother called, Zib. I told her: adequate."
3. **The towel text wastes the film's best-known joke on a cruise callback.**
   - Catalogue: "The most useful thing an intern can carry. Zib has used it as a cape, a flag and, once, by
     accident, a towel."
   - Vrell: "A towel. Finally, an intern who knows where theirs is."
   - D1 retires the earlier towel and "don't panic" lines in L4, L5 and L7, so the finale owns them.
   - "Reads as cloth at 36 × 24" is too small. Draw the rack and towel near 104 × 110 like the other pickups.
4. **Three souvenirs carry no reference.** Swap:
   - Door knocker → **bowl of petunias**: "Fell from a great height. Its only comment: 'Oh no, not again.'"
   - One-way ticket → **the guidebook**: "Its entire entry on Grimsby reads: 'Mostly damp.'"
   - Thumb badge → **ear fish**: "Pop it in your ear and understand anything. Zib tried it on Form 27-B/6. The
     fish asked to be let out."
   - Keep the complaint stamp and the 42 display.
5. **Moe should bend Marvin's two famous lines.** "Brain the size of a saucer, and they leave me on standby." and
   "Sleep. Don't talk to me about sleep."
6. **Artie's archive is generic and breaks the retired Tuesday gag.**
   - Description: "Lay down in front of the demolition fleet in his dressing gown. Fell asleep there. The fleet
     still isn't sure who won."
   - at10: "Asked us not to come on Thursdays. He never could get the hang of Thursdays."
7. **Vogon poetry should be worse, and vary.** Rotate three:
   - "O moist beige form, thy staples gleam / like damp regret in gravy's dream."
   - "Ode to a bollard: thou art grey. / So am I. So is today."
   - "Thy town is in the way, alas. / Please move it. Signed, the underpass."
8. **Memo joke.** Replace with "The notice was on display: our basement, bottom drawer, behind a sign reading
   BEWARE OF COMPLIANCE." Sign-off: "— Vrell. Don't panic. There's a form for that."
9. **Dressing gown line is muddled.** "Artie has faced the end of the world in one twice. He says it's the
   pockets."
10. **Continuity:** Bubbles forwards the notice from a 2199 terminal but has "already left the tank"; say plainly
    where she is. The French L5 copy already uses the fish farewell verbatim, which spends panel 2 (D1 covers the
    rewrite).
11. **Keep:** the poetry-sighting line, the deadline report, all three Gazette headlines, Artie's "Tea first. Then
    the end of the world.", Moe's "An unexpected lift. How disappointing." and his "off switch labelled LATER"
    (it now echoes Vrell's drawer; keep both as a deliberate pair).

**Callbacks.** Krix, Bubbles and the ring all pay off; add at most two.

- Complaint stamp text: "Complaint No. 1, filed by a Gary of Maple Court, re: 'hubcap'. Status: pending."
- Moe at3 closes the cake arc from #0003: "Still holds a wedding cake for a Dean & Sharon. Collection overdue by
  170 years. He admires their commitment."
