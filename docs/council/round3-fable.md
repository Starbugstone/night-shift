## #0003 · Baker Mabel's First Batch

Ideas, References and Callbacks are at 10. Three lines hold Humour at 9.

1. **Zib's launch line is not an English idiom.** "Caught me with my hands in it" reads as a mistake; the French
   version has the real joke and the English does not.
   - EN: "I haven't touched a thing and the bakery's already caught me floury-handed."
   - FR: « J'ai encore rien touché et la boulangerie m'a déjà pris la main dans le pétrin. »
2. **Kitchen timer catalogue text reads as a contradiction.** The joke (it wakes everyone but Mabel) needs its middle
   step.
   - EN: "**Kitchen timer.** Wakes the whole street. Not Mabel. She only wakes when it falls off the shelf."
   - FR: « **Minuteur de cuisine.** Il réveille toute la rue. Pas Mabel. Elle ne se réveille que quand il tombe de
     l'étagère. »
3. **Repeat pie line is soft.** "Baking one for the sky" has no picture.
   - EN: "Another pie. Mabel's put a card on the windowsill: ONE PER SAUCER."
   - FR: « Encore une tarte. Mabel a mis un mot sur le rebord : UNE PAR SOUCOUPE. »

## #0004 · While the Hendersons Are Away

Ideas, References and Callbacks are at 10. Three small things hold Humour at 9.

1. **The gnome's repeat line repeats his catalogue text.** Both say he has faced the drive. Keep the catalogue text
   and change the repeat.
   - EN: "He's requested a window seat. And a little umbrella in his drink."
   - FR: « Il a demandé une place côté hublot. Et une petite ombrelle dans son verre. »
2. **Biscuit tin: "Len has never been wrong twice" needs decoding**, because he is wrong twice in the same line.
   - EN: "**Biscuit tin.** Contains bolts. The tin marked BOLTS contains biscuits. Len says the system works."
   - FR: « **Boîte à biscuits.** Contient des boulons. La boîte marquée BOULONS contient des biscuits. Len dit que le
     système fonctionne. »
3. **Unfound hint is the only line in the level with no voice.** The memo already says he is sulking.
   - EN: "Sulking by Len's east drive."
   - FR: « Il boude près de l'allée de Len, côté est. »

## 1122 · Ni!

Ideas and Callbacks are at 10. Humour and References are at 9.

1. **The film's most quoted line has an empty slot waiting for it** (References). A sighting sends Zib fleeing to the
   checkpoint, and the doc gives Zib no sighting line. Add one, used for any detector:
   - EN: "Run away! Run away!"
   - FR: « Fuyez ! Fuyez ! » (check against the French dub before locking).
2. **Zib's launch line ends on "is this what you meant by half?"**, which only works if the player remembers the
   memo sign-off word for word (Humour). Make the expenses link plain.
   - EN: "They don't have horses. Just coconuts. Vrell, I'd like to expense the other half."
   - FR: « Ils n'ont pas de chevaux. Juste des noix de coco. Vrell, je voudrais me faire rembourser l'autre moitié. »
3. **Shrubbery description misses the line everyone knows** (References). "Tasteful" is a paraphrase of "a nice one,
   not too expensive".
   - EN: "The knights wanted a nice one. Not too expensive. The beam took it for free."
   - FR: « Les chevaliers en voulaient un joli. Pas trop cher. Le rayon l'a eu gratuitement. »
4. **Enchanter's horns is still the flattest of the six items** (Humour). Tie it to Tom's one skill.
   - EN: "Ram's horns on a skullcap. Slightly singed. Everything Tom owns is slightly singed."
   - FR: « Des cornes de bélier sur une calotte. Un peu roussies. Tout ce que possède Tom est un peu roussi. »

## 1953 · The War of the Colds

The staging is now strong: the sneeze in front of the screen, "Bless you!", the ice pack, the bipod status and the
biscuits are all quotable. The scores are held down by the payoff line and three smaller lines.

1. **The thermometer first clear lost its punchline in the trim** (Callbacks 8, Humour). As written, "Temperatures:
   none by mouth" is telegraphic, and "I told Compliance we had no idea" has nothing to refer to because "that's where
   humans got the idea" was cut. The French says something different again ("Par derrière") and never mentions
   temperatures. A cold player gets no joke, and a player who knows the L6 memo gets no answer. Put the punchline back
   and move Compliance to the cover-story row, which is already reserved for Form 7-B. Both lines fit the
   135-character budget (129 and 131).
   - First clear, EN: "Forty temperatures with Grandpa Doyle's brass thermometer. None by mouth. The drive-in watched.
     That's where humans got the idea."
   - First clear, FR: « Quarante températures au thermomètre en laiton de papy Doyle. Aucune par la bouche. Le drive-in
     a tout vu. L'idée leur vient de là. »
   - Cover-story row, EN: "Fever log adopted as Form 7-B. Compliance was told we have no idea where humans got it."
   - Cover-story row, FR: « Relevé de température adopté comme formulaire 7-B. On a dit à la Conformité qu'on ignorait
     d'où les humains tenaient ça. »
2. **The projector sighting line is about the tripod, not about being seen** (Humour, Ideas). It plays when the
   audience spots Zib. Keep the 3-D joke and point it at the saucer.
   - EN: "They spotted me and clapped. They think I'm the 3-D."
   - FR: « Ils m'ont repéré et ils applaudissent. Ils croient que c'est moi, la 3D. »
3. **Clive's second mumble has no joke, and the film's best-known image is unused** (References). The cylinder
   unscrewing is the moment everyone remembers.
   - EN: "It's only a meteor… meteors don't unscrew…"
   - FR: « Ce n'est qu'un météore… ça ne se dévisse pas, un météore… »
4. **Sylvie's second mumble: the French is funnier than the English.** "Not you, sir" loses the logic that "you first"
   has.
   - EN: "Row by row… you first, sir, you're on fire…"
   - FR: keep as written.
5. **The queue behind the screen can set up the first clear without spoiling it** (Ideas, art only). Draw the sick
   pilots in the queue holding their hoods' back hatches shut, with rookie Vrell and his clipboard at the front. No
   thermometer in view and no text. The first-clear line then explains a picture the player has already seen.
6. **Meteor fragment text is the weakest item** (Humour). Its second sentence is a plot summary.
   - EN: "The biggest thing ever to hit Grimsby. The drive-in billed it as a coming attraction."
   - FR: « La plus grosse chose jamais tombée sur Grimsby. Le drive-in l'a annoncée comme "prochainement". »

## 2199 · Your Town Is in the Way

Ideas, References and Callbacks are at 10. The finale panels land, and the four film anchors each have their own
moment. Two lines hold Humour at 9.

1. **Poetry sighting line is plain** (Humour). The film's own consequence of the poetry is the airlock.
   - EN: "He started the second stanza. I asked for the airlock instead."
   - FR: « Il a attaqué la deuxième strophe. J'ai demandé le sas. »
2. **Penny's two mumbles are the same hitchhiking joke** (Humour, "vary the jokes"). Keep "Any planet's fine… not this
   one…" and make the other about her guide, which the room shows on its screen.
   - EN: "One star… no… half…"
   - FR: « Une étoile… non… une demie… »
3. **Optional, art only: close the toaster.** In panel 1, put Krix's toaster on the desk and pop it as his pen hovers
   over "Received". No text. It ends the thread shipped since L1 and costs one retained shape.
