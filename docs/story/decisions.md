# Story decisions (coordinator, 2026-11-25)

The user gave full artistic liberty within the main rules (AGENTS.md hard rules, tone, obvious references, varied jokes,
running-joke cap, era levels built on one film, Grimsby levels generic). These settle the open questions in
[continuity-review.md](continuity-review.md). Every level agent follows them.

- **Era order is free.** We are time travellers, so the Dispatch levels needn't be chronological. Play order is decided by story and
  difficulty, not by date. Each era level must make sense in any order it is played.
- **D1 · The *Hitchhiker's Guide* references belong to the 2199 finale.** Rewrite the shipped L4, L5 and L7 lines that use them (the
  DON'T PANIC billboard, Nigel's "42" towel, the "don't panic" tip, the sardine-tin "So long…" line; the towel swan stays a towel joke
  without the quote) so that the finale owns "So long, and thanks for all the fish", DON'T PANIC, 42 and the towel.
- **D2 · Keep Crest Valley.** Add one line: it's the next valley over from Grimsby, and the Gazette covered it in 1955 ("From our
  archives"). Keep the Grimsby Gazette masthead with the 1955 archive treatment.
- **D3 · One season, ending in 2199.** L8 is the climax of Act III ("Adequate"); 2199 is the season finale. Don't call L8 a
  "season finale" in new copy.
- **D4 · Era levels stay on their film.** Specimen addenda and souvenir texts for era specimens use their own film or personal jokes.
  At most one outside reference per era level, and only if it is instantly obvious. Grimsby specimens keep the mixed-reference addenda
  the user approved.
- **D5 · Zib's Mum pays off in Vrell's 2199 first-clear line:** "Your mother called, Zib. I told her: adequate." (adapt as needed).
- **D6 · Vrell's pension vests in 2199, and he files his retirement form in the drawer marked "Later".** He stays on as Zib's mentor.
- **Brass thermometer:** GDD-13's tripod cold is canonical. Remove the L7 line that spoils it, and pay it off once, at the 1953 first clear.
- **Parking ring:** the rank flavour becomes "Parking ring: waitlisted. Behind Bubbles." Zib gets the ring in 2199.
- **Birch Lane shifts:** Zib is not paid yet ("your first live call"). Introduce Mrs. Pruitt and Gary in #0004 cleanly, so L3 builds on
  them rather than meeting them.
- **Work order numbers:** pad the number in code, not `#000{num}` in strings; key the "Agent Zib" address on the level ID, not the index.
- **Retired gags stay retired** (new "Tuesday" jokes).
- **D7 · Unlocks for the era levels:** once L9 (1955) is delivered, 1122 and 1953 can be played in either order. 2199, the season
  finale, opens once both are delivered, because it closes Krix's and Bubbles' stories. Era first-clear lines never name the "next"
  date (S24); L9's last line opens the dial ("Why is it blinking two more dates?").
- **Callbacks to the story so far (user, 2026-11-25: "tie in the actual story into future episodes, just for humour and context").**
  Each new level carries a few light callbacks to what the player has already done and met: Doyle's slipper, Larry asleep somewhere,
  Gary's complaints, Mrs. Pruitt's binder, Krix and the toaster, Bubbles in her tank, the odd-sock hoard, souvenirs from earlier levels,
  Gazette headlines that remember past nights. In era levels these can be ancestors, an heirloom, or the same object in another age,
  as long as the level's film still leads. Each callback must make sense cold (setup visible on the screen, or a joke that works
  without it), and they count towards the 2–3 running-joke cap only if they repeat across levels.
