# Night Shift · Temporal Dispatch

> **Superseded on films and order (2026-11-25).** Use the current [1955](GDD-10-1955.md), [1122](GDD-12-1122-grail.md), [1953](GDD-13-1953-tripods.md) and [2199](GDD-14-2199-hitchhiker.md) specs and the [story decisions](story/decisions.md) for films, order and story. The engine, registry and save notes below still apply where the current specs use them.

Build **1953 · Grimsby Mob Docks** first. A waterfront numbers racket gives the saucer a new town to fly through using the current traffic, patrol, camera and room rules. It also pays off the brass thermometer from L6 and the last line of Season 1. The first playable cut is **Cold Storage**, a full three-specimen shift with six exits. The rest of the chapter adds a wedding and a drive-in without requiring outdoor abductions, moving landing rings or new controls.

The other dates on the console are **1122** and **2199**. They're part of the story as soon as L8 is delivered. Patent Valley remains the prehistoric end of the original work-order list and becomes a Dispatch replay entry. The dates below describe a fictional Grimsby. Research and prop references live in [Film references](research/film-references.md).

One delivery unlocks the next paid order in a chapter. All the new paid concepts use the three-specimen shift, two routes per specimen, the shared sunrise clock and Delivered / Ghost / Night owl. A chapter opener starts with one familiar rule in clear air and one readable room obstacle with a holding pocket. If the chapter adds a signature, teach it in an isolated crossing that has a bypass or a forgiving retreat. Show a single prompt from the quiet side and make the practice available from the memo. Place that first encounter on each possible opening approach or in each starting room if players can pick targets freely. Alternate entries earn their risk through a shorter route or better par opportunity. Carry rank, Glorbs and optional upgrades across dates. The player's pressure resets to an opener at each date, with controls and recovery rules carried over.

Opener target over 20+ matched human-model seeds with zero upgrades is **85–100% casual / 90–100% skilled delivery**, following the L3 target. Aim for **80–95% / 90–100%** on the middle order, and **75–90% / 85–100%** on the finale. These are design aims. Use the existing six-seed release floors and [difficulty recipe](../.claude/skills/evaluate-difficulty/SKILL.md) for the release gate, and the L3 route floors for an opener. Avoid making every route a Ghost run. Keep the hardest combinations for short branches and finales. Autopilot must prove every map and every exit. Then drive the first-order journey, a loss and retry, and one complete shift per chapter at 375×690 in the existing [browser recipes](../.claude/skills/verify-night-shift/SKILL.md).

## 1953 · Mob Docks

**Look and play.** The lighthouse and harbour road match Barnacle Bay. Tar-black roofs, blue water, a cream ferry office, curved sedan roofs and honey-yellow warehouse windows separate the map from present Grimsby. A night bookkeeper, a pastry closer and an exhausted dock boss run a protection racket on fish-crate insurance. Reskin traffic headlights as sedan sweeps and fixed cameras as loading-lamp sweeps, steward patrols as night watchmen, and periodic windows as office checks. Inside, keep the current rolling carts, gate cycles, breakables and sleepers. The sample thermometer uses the existing no-kick joint hold if it becomes an objective. Start with painted pier sheds and enclosed cutaways. A projector that casts a bounded detection patch is an optional follow-up using the existing frame or pool logic, with no projectile or combat system.

**Story.** Vrell recognises his own first dispatch number and insists on calling it a calibration error. Zib, now staff, leads while Vrell coaches from the present-day console. The dock ledger shows a cheap early OBELISK filing stamped with a date before the Bureau existed. Cold Storage reveals why the brass thermometer became Form 7-B policy: Vrell used a kitchen calibration tool to prove a crate had been logged as a person. The original Vrell gets a typed signature and a crossed-out expense claim. The mission never requires meeting or replacing him. The drive-in's receipts put the duplicate stamp in 1122. Bubbles audits the crate totals from her tank. Mum leaves a voicemail asking why Zib's payslip is dated before their birthday.

**Spend.** About **20 keys for Cold Storage / 43 for all three**. Add a waterfront ground palette, sedan and watchman skins, and period room dressing. A generic visible skin key on existing traffic and torch definitions may be needed where sprites are fixed in the view. Keep detector math and autopilot unchanged.

## 1122 · Keep & Market

**Look and play.** At Grimsby's harbour bend, a moat, crooked market rooflines and a square bell tower replace the roads and civic block. The castle runs an astonishingly bad lodging house. Torch patrols use steward cones, rampart watchers reuse binocular cones, bats reuse flocks and the drawbridge turns on the existing crane sweep. Rooms use pendulums, gates, castors, drafts and the existing second sleeper. A working dumbwaiter with floor stops or a stairwell that carries noise across floors costs new engine work. A sinusoidal counterweight and local sleeper are the first version's alternatives.

**Story.** Zib follows the stamp to a medieval duplicate of the dock contract. The ferry register shows that the toll was prepaid by a customer in 2199. Krix calls it a licensing arrangement. Vrell calls it a refund with nine centuries of interest. Bubbles translates the harbour weights and Vrell puts Zib down as lead investigator. The lodge cat signs the night rota with a paw print.

**Spend.** **24 / 51 keys**. Map theme and reskins first. A true stopped carrier or multi-floor acoustic graph belongs to a later tower project and needs route, retry and timing proof. The 1122 date also lets French copy echo *Les Visiteurs* in passing. The chapter's jokes play as bad hotel service and a toll dispute in both languages.

## 2199 · Orbital Grimsby

**Look and play.** The old harbour bend is the outline of a ring station's shipyard. Navy void, ivory airlock roofs and restrained cool signage leave room for the mint, amber and red game tells. Drones reuse searchlights or CCTV cones, transit shuttles reuse traffic and crane gates, and audit shutters reuse timed gates and room cameras. Use artificial gravity for the first two orders. The optional signature for the finale is a bounded drift chamber with a clearly marked normal-gravity staging bay outside it. Zero gravity needs a gravity parameter with normal defaults, revised beam/autopilot settlement and retry tests. Use the existing lateral swell/draft model if zero gravity misses its proof. ## Dispatch, saves and story triggers

- Keep **Grimsby · Now** and **Temporal Dispatch** as two large Work Orders buttons. Before L8, an unavailable console can hint at the basement. The L8 first-clear report plays the existing **Adequate** finale and offers **Open Dispatch**. The player can always get there later from Work Orders. The Dispatch list shows one large card per era with the date, place and delivered count. Show 1122 and 2199 as signals until their content and prerequisites are available. A chapter opens to its own order list, with a link back to Dispatch and a clear action for the next open order. Return to that list from memo, report and abort. Patent Valley's replay leads to the existing L8 memo.
- Keep one canonical player profile. Move the registry from a global index-based next card to stable chapter/order IDs, preserving `level1` through `level8`. Use explicit prerequisites for each shipped chapter and ID-based lookup for routes, rewards and post-report destinations. Persist best time, stars, attempts and delivery per order. Keep rank thresholds and global Glorbs, report counts, upgrades, preferences and debug unlock on the same profile. Compute unlocks and progress counts from validated deliveries and the shipped catalog. Keep chapter UI selection as a preference with a fallback to Grimsby. Store small, stable first-clear beat IDs for one-time story playback, separately from delivery, and derive paid progress from the run result. An existing L8 finisher gets Dispatch on the first visit after migration. Treat inherited deliveries as seen for automatic story playback and offer a finale replay from the L8 memo.
- For rollout, write a version 2 profile at `night-shift-save-v2`, importing from `night-shift-save-v1` when v2 is absent. The current v1 writer validates a fixed list and would discard the new fields on a same-key write. Separate keys preserve the canonical new profile if a player visits a build with the old writer. Copy the validated progress, rank inputs, Glorbs, preferences, report counts and upgrades, including the existing one-time retired-upgrade refund. Do that on the first successful v2 write. Leave v1 readable by old builds. The two profiles advance independently after import. Resetting in the new build writes a fresh v2 profile so the next load cannot resurrect the imported progress. Test clean starts, malformed data, reset and reload, legacy skipped orders and completed L8, a locked or absent selected chapter, and v1/v2 visiting the same origin. - Use the existing portrait input and HUD. Static scene props remain outside detector and collision lists unless an order explicitly assigns them a hazard. Keep terminal labels, Vrell's brief and hazard prompts in EN and FR. Rank and all chapter controls should fit with French strings. Use report, memo and loading lines for most story. First-clear beats need a short skip affordance and must never run during a timed shift.
